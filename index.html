Why it exists

Take the question into the field.

Cosmic Nexus is for the delicious moment when an old carving, a shipwreck rumour, a sky report, a museum cabinet or a strange family story makes everyone lean forward. The work is to keep that spark alive while building the boring-but-vital parts underneath: maps, dates, source trails, permission notes, travel routes, friendship circles and clear labels for what is known, guessed, fictional or still waiting for review.

Map the trail What is being described, where did it come from, who can check it, and what confidence level does it deserve?
Choose the lane History, myth, UAP, film, travel, legal memory, civic design, sensorium prototype or pure story seed.
Find the people Private notes stay private. Public adventures need hosts, reviewers, local knowledge, correction paths and people who can become friends, not data points.
